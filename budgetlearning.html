Know Where Your Money Goes

01

Income

Money received from an allowance, scholarship, internship, or work.

02

Fixed Expenses

Costs that are usually stable, such as regular fees.

03

Variable Expenses

Costs that change, such as food and transportation.

04

Needs

Essential spending for living, studying, and responsibilities.

05

Wants

Optional purchases that can often be delayed.

06

Savings

Money kept for a future goal or unexpected expense.

07 · MONEY MISTAKES

Recognize Habits That Drain Money

Impulse buying

Wait before making non-essential purchases and check your budget first.

Ignoring small expenses

Track small daily purchases because they can become a large monthly total.

Late payments

Plan payment dates ahead of time to avoid avoidable costs.

Unused subscriptions

Review recurring payments and cancel services you do not use.

Spending without a plan

Create a simple spending plan before using your income.
