04 · LEARN TO MANAGE MONEY

Money Management Guide

Making money is important, but knowing how to manage it wisely is what helps you build a better financial future.

1. Plan Before Spending Future Income

Before receiving your salary or expected income, decide how much you will spend, save and use to settle important financial responsibilities.

2. Think Before Spending Unexpected Money

When you receive a bonus, gift or unexpected payment, avoid rushing to spend it. Review your needs, outstanding debts and savings goals first.

3. Avoid Impulse Spending

Before buying something, ask yourself: Do I really need it? Can I afford it? Will this purchase affect my savings?

4. Build an Emergency Fund

Put some money aside regularly for emergencies such as unexpected repairs, medical expenses or sudden changes in income.

5. Review Your Expenses Regularly

Keep track of where your money goes. Identify unnecessary spending and look for ways to improve your financial habits.

Remember

Good money management is not about how much you earn. It is also about how wisely you use what you have.
