Understanding Needs & Wants

Learning how to distinguish between needs and wants is an important step towards making better financial decisions and managing your money wisely.

What Are Needs?

Needs are things we must have to live a healthy, safe and comfortable life. They are essential expenses that should be prioritised when planning a budget.

Examples of Needs

  • Food and drinking water
  • House rent or accommodation
  • Basic clothing
  • Transportation to work or school
  • Healthcare and medication
  • Electricity and essential bills

Remember: Needs should generally come before unnecessary spending.

What Are Wants?

Wants are things we would like to have but can live without. They may make life more enjoyable, but they are not essential for survival.

Examples of Wants

  • Designer clothes
  • Luxury wristwatches
  • Expensive jewellery
  • Eating at expensive restaurants
  • Upgrading a phone that still works
  • Unnecessary entertainment subscriptions

Remember: It is okay to enjoy your wants, but plan for them after your needs.

Pause Before You Purchase

Before spending your money, take a moment to ask yourself a few important questions.

1. Do I really need this item?

2. Can I afford it without affecting my essential expenses?

3. Am I buying it because I need it or because I want it?

4. Can this money be used for something more important?
